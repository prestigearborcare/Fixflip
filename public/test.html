Who answers my question?

AI handles basic troubleshooting first. Questions it cannot resolve are forwarded, with the conversation, to an industry expert for review. You will see whether forwarding succeeded. Expert replies come by email; there is no guaranteed response time.

How do monthly projects work?

You can open five separate repair issues during each monthly billing period. Related follow-ups use the same project. Projects reset with the next paid billing period; unused slots do not carry over. Earlier conversations remain readable.

How do I cancel?

Open Manage billing, sign in with the email used at checkout, and cancel your subscription. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Your paid access continues through the current billing period.
