FIXFLIP service terms

FIXFLIP is operated by Prestige Arbor Care LLC. Contact fixfliponline@gmail.com for support, billing or refund requests.

The service

FIXFLIP provides remote, AI-generated repair guidance. AI can be wrong. Unresolved questions are submitted for review by an industry expert when forwarding succeeds. No repair outcome, diagnosis, professional credential, response time or on-site service is promised. Parts and repairs are not included.

Prices and project limits

Single Repair Question costs $19.99 once and includes one distinct issue plus related follow-ups, up to 20 customer messages. Project Partner costs $79 per month and adds five repair project credits per paid monthly billing period. Each credit starts one distinct project, with up to 20 customer messages in its starting billing period. Follow-ups must relate to the original issue. Unused project credits carry forward without a cap while the same subscription stays active. Credits are earned from confirmed paid monthly invoices, not simply from time passing. Closing a case does not restore a credit. Unused credits expire when the subscription ends; they have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Earlier-period conversations are read-only.

Renewal and cancellation

The monthly plan is charged at signup and renews monthly at $79 until canceled. Use Manage billing to stop the next renewal. Access remains through the paid period. Cancellation does not automatically refund prior payments. If the service fails or a charge appears wrong, contact us for a refund review. Applicable consumer rights are not limited by these terms.

Safety and access

Do not use FIXFLIP for emergencies. Stop unsafe work and use qualified local help when necessary. Keep your checkout confirmation link private: it grants access to your purchased workspace. We may rate-limit abusive use. Do not submit secrets, payment-card data or private documents in a repair conversation.
